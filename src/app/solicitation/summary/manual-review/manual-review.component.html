Recommended Additions

Add to Section L - Instructions to Offerors:

L.X Section 508 Compliance Requirements

All deliverables must comply with Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act (29 U.S.C. 794d) and the Architectural and Transportation Barriers Compliance Board Electronic and Information Technology (EIT) Accessibility Standards (36 CFR Part 1194). The contractor shall:

  • Ensure all electronic content and communications meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA success criteria
  • Provide Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT) for all proposed software
  • Include accessibility testing in quality assurance processes
  • Document remediation procedures for any accessibility issues identified
  • Maintain accessibility throughout product updates and maintenance

Reference: https://www.section508.gov/

Implementation Steps:

  1. Add Section 508 requirements under technical evaluation criteria
  2. Include accessibility testing in acceptance criteria
  3. Require VPAT submission with technical proposals
  4. Add accessibility metrics to performance requirements
