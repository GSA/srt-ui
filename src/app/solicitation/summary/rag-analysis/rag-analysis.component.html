SECTION 508 ACCESSIBILITY COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS

52.239-40 ACCESSIBILITY OF ELECTRONIC AND INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY (JUN 2001)

(a) The contractor shall ensure that all Electronic and Information Technology (EIT) supplies and services provided under this contract comply with the accessibility standards set forth in 36 CFR Part 1194, Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 (29 U.S.C. 794d), as amended.

(b) ICT Products and Services: All Information and Communication Technology (ICT) products must conform to the Revised Section 508 Standards (36 CFR Part 1194) and Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0 Level AA success criteria.

(c) Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT): The contractor shall provide a completed VPAT 2.4 (or most current version) for each ICT product or service delivered under this contract.

(d) Accessibility Testing: All ICT deliverables must undergo accessibility testing using both automated tools and manual evaluation methods.

(e) Support Documentation and Services: All user manuals, training materials, help documentation, and customer support services must be provided in accessible formats.

(f) Interface Accessibility: Digital interfaces must provide keyboard navigation alternatives, sufficient color contrast ratios (minimum 4.5:1), compatibility with assistive technologies, and adjustable font sizes.

(g) Remediation Requirements: The contractor must remediate any accessibility issues at no additional cost to the Government within 30 days of notification.

(h) Compliance Verification: The Government reserves the right to conduct independent accessibility testing of all ICT deliverables.
