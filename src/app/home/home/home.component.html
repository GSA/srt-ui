Section 508 Integration Assessment

Our AI-powered scanner reviews your solicitation packages to determine whether Section 508 accessibility requirements are properly referenced and incorporated into the solicitation language.

  • accessibility_new

    Section 508 Integration Check

    Reviews each solicitation document for the language, clauses, and references that show Section 508 accessibility requirements have been built into the procurement (such as FAR 39.2, the relevant Section 508 standards, and accessibility deliverables). The result tells you whether the solicitation includes that language, doesn't include it, or doesn't need to.

  • summarize

    Solicitation Report Summary

    Generates a comprehensive AI analysis of the entire solicitation package, summarizing integration posture and key findings.
